For Linux, on a mini PC or a Raspberry Pi

Astrophotography from your browser.

NOVA runs on a small computer at the telescope and controls your mount, cameras, focuser, filter wheel, rotator and flat panel through INDI. You use it from a browser on any computer, tablet or phone on the same network, with nothing to install there.

Install NOVA Source on GitHub

Free and open source (GPL 3). Inspired by N.I.N.A.

A whole night, from one page

Everything runs on the computer at the telescope, so the work carries on when your laptop sleeps or the Wi-Fi drops.

Imaging

Exposures with cooling, gain, offset and binning. Stretched colour previews, with stars, HFR and FWHM for every frame. An image history to compare side by side or blink. Optional live stacking.

Plate solving

Sync, slew and centre on a target to within an arcminute with ASTAP, and see the stars named on solved frames.

Targets

Offline catalogues, tonight's best targets for your site, a sky chart with survey imagery, framing with rotation and mosaics, and the sky at any other time to plan before it's dark.

Sequences

A night unattended over several targets: centring, autofocus with filter offsets, guiding, dithering, meridian flips, flats, the camera's rotation matched for each target, and a stop at dawn.

Guiding

NOVA's own guider, with predictive PEC for strain-wave mounts, Dec backlash compensation and a guiding assistant. Or PHD2, running on the mini PC and shown in a tab.

Focus and polar alignment

V-curve autofocus, and polar alignment by three points or with the camera on the pole, with the error shown live as you turn the knobs.

Your equipment

INDI drivers chosen on the page, profiles to switch between setups, and ZWO AM3, AM5 and AM7 mounts over USB or Bluetooth.

Built for the field

No internet needed. A red night mode. Its own Wi-Fi hotspot when the site has none. Frames saved to the mini PC or straight to a USB drive, and a message to your phone when the night is done.

A look around

Made for phones and tablets too

On a touch screen NOVA switches to a compact layout: the image fills the screen and the controls float over it. Added to the Home Screen, it opens like an app.

Install

A used ex-office mini PC is cheap, fast and ideal, and small 12 V ones can ride on the telescope. A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 works too. See Choosing a computer.

On a 64-bit Arch, Debian 13, Raspberry Pi OS (trixie) or Ubuntu 26.04 system:

sudo apt install git             # Arch: sudo pacman -S --needed git
git clone https://github.com/geoffpedder/nova-astro.git && cd nova-astro
deploy/setup.sh

setup.sh installs INDI and your equipment's drivers, the ASTAP plate solver, PHD2 and NOVA itself. Then open http://<name>.local:5000, where a Getting started checklist takes you through the rest.

Later, nova update installs new releases and nova doctor checks everything.
